Kurt Svensson

1953 — 2026

A Life Well Lived

Kurt Svensson passed away peacefully in his beloved Stockholm at the age of 73. A true son of Sweden, Kurt was known for his quiet wisdom, his love for the Baltic sea, and his unwavering kindness to neighbors and strangers alike.

Throughout his seven decades, Kurt witnessed the changing landscape of his city, yet he remained a constant pillar for his family. Whether he was strolling through Gamla Stan or enjoying a quiet fika by the water, he found beauty in the simple rhythms of life.

"Deeply missed, forever remembered, and always in our hearts."
